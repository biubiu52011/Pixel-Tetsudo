## N. DO NOT FIX LIST

This is the most important section of this audit.
Knowing what NOT to touch is more valuable than knowing what to fix.

### DO NOT FIX — Legal architectural boundaries
1. Three-layer data architecture (UNIFIED_LINES / DataLayer / RailwayDB)
   Reason: Intentional separation of raw data, runtime cache, and display identity
2. DataLayer.getLine delegating to RailwayDB.getLine
   Reason: Proper layering — DataLayer adds runtime capabilities on top of RailwayDB
3. Multiple escapeHtml/t/init definitions across modules
   Reason: Self-contained module utilities, no cross-module dependency
4. tOp local wrappers in data-state.js and realtime-view.js
   Reason: Thin delegators, not duplicate implementations

### DO NOT FIX — Intentional compat fallbacks
5. UNIFIED_LINES fallback in data-fusion.js, route-search.js, trains-page.js
   Reason: Graceful degradation when DataLayer/RailwayDB unavailable

   Reason: Sandbox/test environment support
