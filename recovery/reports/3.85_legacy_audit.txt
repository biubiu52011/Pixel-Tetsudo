  File: js/data-state.js:237
  Code: escapeHtml(op) where op = line.operator (e.g. JR-East, TokyoMetro, Seibu)

  Should use: tOp(op) which is already defined at data-state.js:53-55
  Impact: All 156 lines show raw operator IDs in Trains page
  Verdict: FIX - use tOp(op) instead of escapeHtml(op)
