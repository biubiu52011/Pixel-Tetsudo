=== P2-2: tLineName NEVER DEFINED ===

Current behavior:
  data-state.js renderCard(): displayName = tLineName(line) || nameEn || name || lineId
  realtime-view.js getDisplayName(): nameJa || tLineName(line) || nameEn || name || id

Problem: tLineName is NEVER assigned anywhere.
  - grep across all JS files: no window.tLineName = ... found
  - No definition in lang-init.js, translations.js, or common.js

Impact on data-state.js (Trains cards):
  Falls through to line.nameEn → shows internal IDs like:
    SeibuIkebukuro (should be 西武池袋線)
    ChuoRapid (should be 中央線快速)
    Joban (should be 常磐快速線)

Impact on realtime-view.js (Realtime modal):
  nameJa is checked FIRST → shows correct Japanese names
  tLineName is never reached because nameJa exists for all lines

ROOT CAUSE: tLineName was intended as a translation bridge but was never implemented.
          realtime-view.js avoided the bug by checking nameJa first.
          data-state.js never got the same fix.

PROPOSED FIX: Change data-state.js displayName to match realtime-view.js pattern:
  nameJa || nameEn || name || lineId
  (Remove the tLineName call entirely — it was a dead variable)
